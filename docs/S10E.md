# Samsung Galaxy S10e

The Galaxy S10e is this fork's current primary development and test device.
The focus is Android gaming, particularly Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB),
with a keyboard/mouse workflow in landscape orientation.

## Landscape editor workflow

Use the mapping editor and settings in landscape when configuring the gaming
workflow. The original layout clipped editor/settings controls on small
landscape displays, limiting access to those controls.

[PR #1](https://github.com/rickallauigan/XtMapper/pull/1) includes the scrollable
editor/settings fix. Editor chrome respects safe insets and the display cutout,
while `keyContainer` remains the fullscreen mapping coordinate surface.
Its mapping coordinates were intentionally left unchanged. This UI change
makes editor/settings controls accessible without redefining mapping positions.

## Current validation

- XtMapper builds successfully in our environment.
- It installs and runs on the S10e.
- Keyboard and mouse are usable with our XtMapper setup.
- The small-landscape UI fix was manually tested on the S10e.

The [MLBB KBM S10e v0.1 profile](../profiles/mlbb/samsung-s10e/README.md) was
physically verified with the user in 1v1 Hero Training: movement and diagonals,
quick and aimed skills, utilities, mouse buttons, camera, simultaneous inputs,
and normal touchscreen use. Hardware: rooted SM-G970F / beyond0lte, Android 16
LineageOS, Evision RGB Keyboard and Lenovo Multi-function Mouse M300.
The mapping plane is 2280×1080, origin 0,0; the left cutout is never a mapping
offset. The documented Dyrroth HUD and Revitalize spell are the calibration
source. Other heroes, HUD layouts, peripherals, and Android versions need testing.

Use XtMapper(Debug), `xtr.keymapper.debug`, with **My Gaming Setup** bound to
`com.mobile.legends` → **MLBB KBM S10e v0.1**, automatic profiling enabled.
Camera inspection stays held while stationary; mouse inactivity during D-pad
movement releases the camera for hero-follow. Battlefield drag travel is
limited by available screen space.
